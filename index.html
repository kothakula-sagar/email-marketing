Email deliverability checklist · CSV import format

Responsible sending

Keep a record of opt-in. Use a recognizable sender name, accurate subject, and business address. The app adds an unsubscribe email link and reply instructions. Check the mailbox and record opt-outs promptly.

Ask your mail or domain administrator to configure SPF (authorized senders), DKIM (message signing), and DMARC (domain alignment and policy). Confirm these records and run a small test before a larger campaign.

Review the rules that apply to your recipients and organization, including US CAN-SPAM, EU GDPR and related electronic marketing rules, and India’s DPDP framework and applicable commencement dates. This is general guidance. Consult a qualified professional for legal advice.

Install on Windows

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11
  • Node.js 22.12 or newer
  • An approved SMTP mailbox and its outgoing settings

  1. Open the project folder

    Extract the ZIP. Open the folder containing package.json, press Alt+D, type cmd, and press Enter.

  2. Create your private configuration

    if not exist .env copy .env.example .env
notepad .env

    Enter your mailbox settings and replace the example email addresses, sender details, and business address. Save and close Notepad.

  3. Install and start

    npm install
npm start

    Keep the terminal open and visit localhost:3000 on your PC. Confirm the mailbox is configured before sending.

Troubleshooting

Port 3000 is already in use

Close the original app window with Ctrl+C, or set APP_PORT=3001 in .env, restart, and open localhost:3001. A new port uses a separate browser storage origin.

SMTP authentication failed

Confirm the SMTP username, provider-specific password, and host. Check that your provider permits password-based SMTP, and restart after editing .env.

Node.js version is too old

Run node --version. Install Node.js 22.12 or newer, then reopen Command Prompt.

Frequently asked questions

Is Email Campaign Studio free?

Yes. The app has no monthly fee. Your email provider may charge for the mailbox or its sending plan.

Does it work with Gmail or Outlook SMTP?

Gmail works where Google allows an app password. Outlook.com requires OAuth, which this release does not implement. Microsoft 365 compatibility depends on your organization allowing password-based SMTP submission.

How many emails can I send per day?

The app defaults to 50 accepted sends per day per running process and up to 10 recipients per batch. Your provider may apply lower limits or additional restrictions.

Where is my data stored?

Contacts, drafts, and send history stay in the browser profile you use with the local app. The SMTP provider receives the recipient address and message when you send.

Is it a SaaS or a desktop app?

It is a local Windows workflow. You start a Node.js server on your PC and open the app in your browser at localhost:3000.

How do I back up contacts?

Export a customer CSV from Customers, or download a full JSON backup from Settings to include contacts, campaigns, and send history. Restore the JSON backup from Settings.

How does unsubscribe work?

Messages include an unsubscribe email link and reply instructions. Mark the address unsubscribed in Customers when a request arrives. The app excludes that address from campaign sends.

Can I send to purchased lists?

No. Send only to recipients who have opted in. Importing a list or labeling a row Subscribed does not establish consent.

What is the difference from Mailchimp?

Email Campaign Studio stores campaign data in your browser and uses your SMTP mailbox. Mailchimp is a hosted service with account-based plans and its own sending infrastructure.
