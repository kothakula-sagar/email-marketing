Email deliverability checklist

Review this checklist before sending your first campaign and whenever your sender changes.

Before sending · After sending

Before sending

  1. Confirm opt-in and remove opted-out or invalid addresses.
  2. Check your sender identity and SPF, DKIM, and DMARC with your provider.
  3. Verify the business address and unsubscribe reply address.
  4. Send to a selected test audience. Inspect the subject, links, layout, and unsubscribe instructions.

After sending

Review the app’s accepted and failed results, then check your mailbox for bounces and unsubscribe requests. Mark opt-outs in Customers. SMTP acceptance can precede a later bounce.
