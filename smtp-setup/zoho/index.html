Zoho Mail SMTP setup

Configure the Zoho Mail SMTP host, TLS port and application password for your account.

Connection settings

AccountSMTP_HOSTSMTP_PORTEncryption
Personal or eligible free organizationsmtp.zoho.com587 or 465587 STARTTLS · false / 465 implicit TLS · true
Paid organization custom domainsmtppro.zoho.com587 or 465587 STARTTLS · false / 465 implicit TLS · true

Authentication

These are Zoho’s listed hosts for its .com data center. Use the host shown in your account for your region. Enter your full mailbox address as SMTP_USER and SENDER_EMAIL. Use an application-specific password when required by your two-factor settings.

Save your settings in .env, then restart the app. See the Windows setup guide.

Troubleshooting

  • Check your account’s regional host and SMTP access.
  • If login fails, check which password the account requires.
  • If the sender is rejected, confirm it matches SMTP_USER and is approved by Zoho.
